Home

As a scientist, one of the major challenges, besides meaningful research itself, is to bring your research to the community. To overcome this challenge, I wanted to make research topics easier accessible through R packages. In this process, I created pmxNODE, a package to facilitate the implementation of low-dimensional NODEs in pharmacometric software, and optiDoseR, a package to generate code for the optiDose approach in pharmacometric software.

This website is dedicated to present tutorials for these packages and other topics of my research.

If you’re interested in my research topics and want to apply some methods in your projects or would like to tailor my R packages to your workflow, feel free to reach out any time.

My research is based on three pillars:

ML in PMX
Harness the power of data to accelerate and refine your pharmacometric analyses. I integrate machine learning techniques to pharmacometrics, making it easier for to uncover insights, improve accuracy, and save time in drug development processes.
Pharmacometrics
Pharmacometrics requires deep knowledge of pharmacology, data analysis, and modeling. With a solid foundation in pharmacokinetics, pharmacodynamics, and clinical data analysis, I’m aiming for robust models and reliable outcomes.
R Programming
R is a powerful tool for data analysis, visualization, and modeling, especially in the life sciences. I aim to streamline workflows and create reproducible analyses with R.
